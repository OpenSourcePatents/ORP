the equivalent AppState-driven GUI run, executed back to back headless: trajectory

## Notes and open items

1. The GUI-wall negation carve-out is spec-mandated signage ("Initial heading (input,
   not target)"); the scanner accepts a forbidden term only immediately after
   not/never/no plus at most one article. Extending the term list stays allowed;
   shrinking it or widening the window stays forbidden.
2. PyQt6 6.11 was installed into the dev environment for this work; it ships only in
   the optional `gui` extra, and nothing outside `orp/gui/` and its tests imports Qt.
3. The session aero-calculator schema gap (app-core addendum, open item 1) is
   unchanged; the GUI saves sessions through the same `save_session` contract.
4. Plot/GUI tests roughly double suite wall time (~3 min on this machine); the heavy
   pieces are the gates re-evaluation (~2 s per call) and eight-figure rendering.
